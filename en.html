Biography

Sheng Xu is a lecturer at the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Communication University of China. He received his Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from Beihang University in June 2024, advised by Prof. Jinhu Lu and Prof. Baochang Zhang. Previously, he worked as an algorithm expert in Alibaba Token Hub at Alibaba Group.

His research focuses on neural network compression and acceleration, low-bit quantization, knowledge distillation, model sparsification, efficient multimodal large models, and real-time interactive video generation. He has coauthored two books and more than 40 academic papers, including over 20 papers in CCF-A / B journals and conferences. His work has received 1,978 Google Scholar citations. His honors include the Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award from Beihang University, Alibaba's Ali Star Talent Program, and the Young Top Talent Program at Communication University of China.
